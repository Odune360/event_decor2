1. Introduction

MC - PETARH ("we", "us", "our") provides event decoration and styling services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website and booking process, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or booking our services, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number
  • Event details — event date, venue name and address, guest count, event type, theme/design preferences, budget range
  • Photos or inspiration images you share with us for your design brief
  • Payment and billing information (processed by [payment processor, e.g. Paystack/Flutterwave/bank transfer] — we do not store full card details)
  • Messages you send us via our contact form, email, WhatsApp, or social media

Information collected automatically

  • Basic website usage data (pages visited, browser type, approximate location from IP address)
  • Cookies used for website functionality and, if applicable, analytics (see Section 4)

Photos and videos from your event

  • With your consent (see our Terms and Conditions), we may photograph or film the decor setup at your event for our portfolio and marketing

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and prepare accurate quotes
  • To plan, confirm, and deliver your event decor booking
  • To process deposits and payments
  • To communicate updates about your booking (confirmations, reminders, changes)
  • To showcase completed work in our portfolio and marketing, where consented to
  • To improve our services and website based on usage patterns
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some website features (like a booking form remembering your entries).

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers — payment processors, website hosting, and, where relevant, delivery or logistics partners helping fulfil your booking
  • Other vendors involved in your event — only with your consent, e.g. sharing venue access details with a co-vendor you've asked us to coordinate with
  • Legal authorities, where required by law
  • Business transfers, if the business is sold or restructured

6. Data Retention

We keep booking and contact information for as long as needed to deliver our services, meet tax/accounting obligations, and handle any post-event follow-up. Portfolio photos may be kept indefinitely for marketing unless you ask us to remove them.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting access to booking and payment details to staff who need it to do their job. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate details
  • Request deletion of your information, once any active booking or legal obligation is complete
  • Ask us to remove your event photos from our public portfolio
  • Opt out of marketing messages at any time
